# Day 5 — Ditter Board Prototype

**Date:** 2026-10-25

## What I worked on

Built [Ditter Board Prototype](https://github.com/LadyHannelore/Ditter-Board-Prototype),
a small browser-based Treasure-Stealing Ditter game. Two apprentice knights move
on a grid, and the match ends when either reaches the marked treasure tile.

## What I learned

I practiced modelling a small board game: selecting a piece, highlighting its
legal adjacent moves, and ending the match when a win condition is met.

## Challenges

Keeping the rules simple: no diagonal moves and no moving onto another piece.
