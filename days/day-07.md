# Day 7 — Gil Merchant Calculator

**Date:** 2026-10-27

## What I worked on

Built [Gil Merchant Calculator](https://github.com/LadyHannelore/Gil-Merchant-Calculator).
It grew from a web calculator into an 8-bit Python (pygame) shop game where
customers need change, coin exchanges, price quotes, margin calculations, or
haggling.

## What I learned

I practiced keeping game rules (`game_logic.py`) separate from rendering
(`main.py`), and turning coin conversion and profit-margin maths into play.

## Challenges

Balancing the haggle rules: a counter must cover cost plus 15%, and too much
greed makes the customer walk away.
