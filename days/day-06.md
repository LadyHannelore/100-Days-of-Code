# Day 6 — Trombe Cellular Automaton

**Date:** 2026-10-26

## What I worked on

Built [Trombe Cellular Automaton](https://github.com/LadyHannelore/Trombe-Cellular-Automaton),
where you click or drag to plant trombe seeds that spread across a grid and
drain the ground gray. A clear mode and a "Clear all" button remove them.

## What I learned

I practiced the update loop of a cellular automaton: each cell's next state
depends on its neighbors, and the grid is redrawn every step.

## Challenges

Letting the player both plant and clear cells while the simulation keeps running.
